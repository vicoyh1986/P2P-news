Ride-hailing leaders: what comes next

A management snapshot of Uber, Grab, Gojek and DiDi, with three moves to watch for each.

Research reviewed 20 Oct 2026

A curated overview, separate from the daily news feed. Targets are company ambitions, not guarantees; ongoing initiatives are labelled and source dates are shown.

Uber Grab Gojek DiDi

Uber

Global ride-hailing, delivery and freight platform.

Leadership
Dara Khosrowshahi, CEO
Core markets
Global, including North America and Europe
Management focus
Use its rider network and fleet partnerships to scale autonomous rides.
Leadership & strategy source · Mar 2026

Three next moves

  1. Bring Lucid + Nuro robotaxis to Houston

    Targeting mid-2027 service, followed by more cities. Uber is preparing charging and fleet facilities.

    Uber announcement · Jun 2026
  2. Scale driverless rides in the Middle East

    With WeRide, aims for at least 1,200 robotaxis across Abu Dhabi, Dubai and Riyadh as soon as 2027, subject to approvals and milestones.

    Uber announcement · Feb 2026
  3. Build a Rivian robotaxi fleet

    Plans an initial 10,000 autonomous R2 vehicles, starting in San Francisco and Miami in 2028. The roadmap targets 25 cities by 2031.

    Uber announcement · Mar 2026

Grab

Ride-hailing, deliveries and digital financial services in one app.

Leadership
Anthony Tan, co-founder & Group CEO
Core markets
Southeast Asia, including Singapore, Malaysia and Indonesia
Management focus
Grow profitably while using AI and automation to improve everyday services.
Leadership & strategy source · May 2026

Three next moves

  1. Expand autonomous mobility with WeRide

    Ongoing regional programme to integrate robotaxis and shuttles into Grab's network, with local testing, approvals and driver retraining.

    WeRide partnership plan · Aug 2025
  2. Give 30,000 partners practical AI skills

    The OpenAI programme aims to train drivers, couriers and merchants over two years, expanding from Singapore to Thailand, Indonesia and the Philippines.

    Grab announcement · Sep 2026
  3. Reach a larger earnings milestone by 2028

    Targets US$1.5 billion in adjusted EBITDA, an earnings measure before interest, tax, depreciation, amortisation and company adjustments; it is not net profit.

    Grab financial target · Mar 2026

Gojek part of GoTo

Motorcycle and car rides, food delivery and on-demand services.

Leadership
Hans Patuwo, CEO of parent company GoTo
Core markets
Indonesia and Singapore
Management focus
Make frequent trips affordable while supporting drivers and protecting margins.
Parent leadership update · Oct 2026

Three next moves

  1. Put affordable, frequent-use services first

    GoTo says Gojek will shift focus toward mass-market products following Indonesia's GoRide commission change. Ongoing priority, not a dated launch.

    GoTo outlook · Jul 2026
  2. Build on locally trained AI

    Parent-company initiative: GoTo released an Indonesian and regional-language AI model in October and is developing local AI capabilities. A Gojek-specific rollout date has not been announced.

    GoTo AI programme · Oct 2026
  3. Continue investing in driver support

    Ongoing expansion of partner benefits, including social security coverage, scholarships and tenure rewards. The programme is already running, rather than a future product launch.

    Gojek programme · Jul 2026

DiDi

China-centred mobility platform, with overseas services including Brazil's 99.

Leadership
Cheng Wei (Will Cheng), founder & CEO
Core markets
China, with major businesses in Brazil and Mexico
Management focus
Improve core ride services while investing in overseas growth and autonomous driving.
Leadership & strategy reporting · Aug 2026

Three next moves

  1. Advance the next-generation robotaxi

    DiDi's R2, developed with GAC Aion, is in road testing. Continued autonomous-driving investment is an ongoing priority; no firm commercial launch date is cited.

    Q2 strategy reporting · Aug 2026
  2. Grow electric mobility in Mexico

    Aims to build a 100,000-EV fleet by 2030, supported by vehicle, charging, financing and insurance partners.

    DiDi Mexico programme · reviewed Oct 2026 (Spanish)
  3. Deepen Brazil's one-app offering

    Ongoing growth of 99's rides, food and instant delivery services. 99 Compras has launched in São Paulo; further expansion has no firm timetable in the cited report.

    Brazil expansion reporting · Aug 2026
